An accelerator · A reality show · A venture fund

On screen, a show. Behind it, a fund.

Every season, ten technology startups at Series A and B spend four weeks together in a new country. We invest in each of them, put specialists inside their companies, and connect them with clients and partners in that country, in the UAE and around the world. Cameras follow the whole journey for a series watched by founders around the world. After sixteen countries, the tour comes home for a two-month finale across the GCC, where the fifteen companies that have grown the most receive follow-on investment. The programme runs inside a regulated ADGM venture fund, so investors get the governance and protection of a licensed structure, and a stake in every company on screen.

What it is, in one minute

Three things, one production

Most accelerators give advice. Most funds write cheques and wait. Most startup content is talk. This brings the three together, and each one makes the others stronger.

01 · The accelerator

Four weeks that move a company

Ten Series A and B tech startups, from any sector, live and work together in a new country. Specialists sit inside each company and do the work with them.

  • USD 100K invested in every company
  • Travel, stay and programme fully paid
  • Clients in the host country, the UAE and worldwide
02 · The reality show

Real founders, real stakes

Every season is filmed as a series. Viewers follow founders racing to win clients before the month ends, in a new country each time.

  • An audience already built: 2M+ views
  • A new destination every season
  • Founders apply because they watched
03 · The fund

The structure investors see

The whole programme sits inside a closed-ended venture fund in Abu Dhabi Global Market, run by a licensed fund manager.

  • Equity in all 160 companies
  • Bought at a discount to their value
  • Regulated, audited and reported

Accelerator + reality show = deal flow, growth and visibility. The fund gives investors ownership, with governance.

For investors

Three ways it pays

The fund pays for everything in a season: the cheques, the travel, the specialists and the filming. In return, each company gives the fund far more equity than a cash investment alone would buy.

01 · At entry
USD 400K of equity for USD 100K

Each company gives the fund USD 400K of equity for a USD 100K cheque plus the programme, at an independent valuation based on its last round.

Fund invests per seasonUSD 2.0M
Equity received per seasonUSD 4.5M
02 · During the fund
Cash back along the way

When the programme brings a startup a client, the startup pays a success commission on that sale for as long as the contract runs. The fund's share is paid out to investors, so cash can come back years before any exit.

Governance

Fun on screen. Rigour behind it.

The fund structure exists to give investors what an accelerator or a production company cannot: regulation, oversight and a clear claim on the upside.

  • Regulated in ADGMClosed-ended venture fund under the FSRA rulebook.
  • Licensed fund managerEvery investment decision sits with the regulated manager.
  • Independent valuationEach company valued independently, starting from its last round.
  • Audit and reportingAnnual audited accounts and regular investor reports.
  • Investor committeeAn advisory committee reviews conflicts and key decisions.
  • Professional investors onlyOffered privately, with a clear prospectus and terms.

The show never sells the fund. On screen we promote the founders, the programme and the countries. The fund is offered privately and separately. That keeps it compliant, and keeps the show real.
